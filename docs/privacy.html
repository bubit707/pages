Privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Your photos, processed on your device. Here is what Mozaik stores locally and how Apple and other services are involved.

1. About this policy

This policy explains how Mozaik handles information when you use the mobile app. Mozaik creates high-resolution photo mosaics from photos you select from your photo library. The App has no developer-operated server for photo processing: mosaic creation and image analysis take place locally on your device.

2. Photos and local processing

The App uses the photos you select, subject to the access you grant through Apple’s photo permissions. Photos and image data used for mosaic processing are not uploaded or sent to the developer or to a developer-operated processing service. We do not receive your photo library or generated mosaics through the App.

3. What the local database stores

For selected photos, the App stores only a 50 × 50 pixel representation and the corresponding photo-library identifier in a local database. The pixel representation helps the App match photos when creating a mosaic; the identifier allows it to locate the corresponding asset in your photo library. These small image representations still contain visual information from your photos, and the identifiers are references to those photos; they should not be treated as anonymous data. The database does not store EXIF metadata, location coordinates, camera information, or other photo metadata. It does not store full-resolution copies of your source photos.

4. How information is used and shared

Local photo data is used to build and create your mosaics. The App does not send this data to us for analytics, advertising, profiling, or tracking, and we do not sell it. If you choose to export, save, or share a mosaic, the destination you select may store or receive that output under its own terms and privacy policy.

5. Photo permissions and your choices

You control the photo access granted to Mozaik in your device’s Settings. You can change or revoke that access at any time. Restricting access, or deleting a photo from your library, may prevent the App from locating it or completing a project. Revoking permission does not itself erase image representations already stored in the App’s local database.

6. Retention, deletion, and backups

The local database remains in the App’s storage until it is removed. To remove the App and its local data from the device, use Delete App rather than Offload App in the device’s storage settings. Deleting Mozaik does not delete source photos or mosaics saved separately in Photos, Files, or another destination, and does not cancel a subscription. Device or iCloud backups may retain app data depending on your Apple settings. Photo-library syncing and downloads, backups, and restored data are handled by Apple and your chosen services; manage those copies through their settings. We cannot access or remotely erase your local database or those backups.

7. Subscriptions and Apple

Mozaik offers two subscription options through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles payment, billing, renewals, cancellation, and refund requests. The App uses subscription entitlement information to determine access to paid features; the image database described above is separate from Apple’s purchase processing. We do not receive your full payment-card details. Apple’s handling of Apple Account, purchase, diagnostic, and other platform information is governed by Apple’s own privacy policy and settings.

8. Security

Keeping image processing on your device limits disclosure to a developer-operated service, but no storage or device can be guaranteed completely secure. Access to local data depends on your device security, operating system, backups, and any destinations you choose for exported files. Keep your device protected and maintain backups of content you want to preserve.

9. This website and third-party links

This policy’s on-device processing statements describe the mobile app. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting it involves requests to GitHub, which may process technical information such as your IP address under its own privacy statement. Following links to Apple or other services is subject to those services’ privacy practices.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the App or its information practices change. The revised date appears above, and we will provide additional notice or request permission where required. For privacy questions, use the developer contact information linked from Mozaik’s App Store listing. Please do not send private photos or photo-library identifiers with a support request unless needed and you choose to do so.
