Help with Mozaik

Frequently asked questions

Getting started, creating mosaics, and making the most of your photos.

What is Mozaik?

Mozaik creates a photographic mosaic by reconstructing a source image from hundreds or thousands of smaller photos. The composition reads as the original image from a distance, while closer inspection reveals the individual photographs used as tiles.

How does Mozaik work?

After you select the photos that may be used as tiles, Mozaik prepares a compact square representation of each image and analyzes its dominant colors.

When you choose a source image, the app divides it into a grid and finds a suitable tile for the color of each position. It then assembles those selections into a single high-resolution image. The entire process takes place on your device.

Why do photos need to be synced first?

Syncing prepares the selected photos for efficient comparison during mosaic generation. Mozaik stores a small square representation and color information for each selection, allowing it to evaluate thousands of possible tile placements without repeatedly processing the full-size originals. Your original photos remain in the Photo Library.

How many photos should I sync?

There is no required number. A larger and more varied collection generally produces better color matching and reduces visible repetition. Collections that include a broad range of subjects, lighting conditions, and colors give Mozaik more suitable choices for each part of the source image.

What do the substitution methods do?

Mozaik always searches your synced library first. The selected substitution method determines what happens when none of your photos is a sufficiently close color match.

Stock Photos may use a built-in image, Single Color uses a solid tile in the required color, and None restricts the mosaic entirely to your synced photos. Choosing None preserves an exclusively personal photo set, but may produce less accurate colors.

How can I change which photos Mozaik can access?

If you granted limited access, open the Sync tab and use Manage to add more photos or open the app's photo permissions in iOS Settings. You can also change access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > Mozaik. After changing access, return to the Sync tab and update your selection.

Why are some photos missing from my mosaic?

Synced photos are candidates rather than guaranteed placements. Mozaik selects tiles according to their color suitability, so some images may not be used in a particular composition. A photo may also become unavailable if it was removed from your library, excluded from limited photo access, or has not yet been downloaded from iCloud.

How does Mozaik reduce repeated tiles?

For each color, Mozaik retains several suitable candidates and varies their placement throughout the composition. It also avoids placing the same photo directly beside or above itself when alternatives are available. Repetition may still occur when only a small number of photos match a particular color range. Syncing a larger and more diverse library provides more alternatives.

What is the color cache?

The color cache records suitable tile candidates for colors Mozaik has already evaluated. Reusing these results reduces repeated analysis and improves the speed of subsequent mosaic generation. The cache is refreshed automatically when you change the synced photo collection or substitution method.

How large can a mosaic be?

At the maximum setting, a 600 × 900 grid using 50-pixel tiles produces a 45,000 × 30,000 image, equivalent to 1.35 billion pixels.

That image represents approximately 5.4 GB of uncompressed pixel data. The saved file is usually smaller, although it may still occupy several gigabytes depending on its colors and detail. Mozaik may require approximately 5.9 GB of available storage before beginning an export at this size. Some apps, websites, and printing services cannot process images of these dimensions.

Why can mosaic files be so large?

A mosaic may contain hundreds of millions, or even more than a billion, pixels. Preserving enough detail for every tile to remain recognizable at close range requires substantially more image data than a typical phone photograph. The final size depends on the grid dimensions, tile size, source material, colors, and visual complexity. Mozaik checks the available storage before generation begins.

How does Mozaik manage very large images?

Mozaik renders very large mosaics in sections instead of keeping the complete image in memory at once. It reuses prepared tile data and displays a smaller preview within the app. This approach allows the app to generate images whose full uncompressed representation would otherwise exceed the device's available memory.

Why does my device get warm?

Generating a large mosaic or video requires sustained image processing and may cause the device to become warm. Mozaik monitors the device's thermal state and reduces or pauses its workload when necessary. If the temperature reaches a critical level, generation stops safely so that it can be attempted again after the device has cooled.

Are my photos safe?

Mozaik processes photos, mosaics, and videos locally and does not upload them to a server. For each synced photo, it stores a 50 × 50 square representation, a reference used to identify the corresponding item in the Photo Library, and analyzed color information. It does not retain full-size originals, location data, or camera metadata.

How does video creation work?

Mozaik can render a finished mosaic as a short animation. Depending on the selected style, you can adjust parameters such as speed and the number of photos revealed at each stage. Video rendering takes place entirely on the device, after which the result can be previewed and saved to the Photo Library.

What is the difference between the subscription plans?

The Free plan supports mosaics up to a 300 × 450 grid and adds a watermark. The No Watermark plan supports the same maximum grid without a watermark. The Ultra Resolution plan removes the watermark and increases the maximum grid to 600 × 900, enabling exports of up to 1.35 gigapixels when using 50-pixel tiles.

How do I restore, manage, or cancel my subscription?

Open Settings in Mozaik and select Subscription. From there, you can restore purchases or open Apple's subscription management interface to change or cancel an active subscription. Purchases and billing are managed through your Apple Account. Cancelling prevents the next renewal and does not remove access before the current paid period ends.

Why can't another app open or share my mosaic?

Very large mosaics can exceed the file-size or image-dimension limits of other apps, websites, cloud services, and printers. This can happen even when Mozaik created and saved the image successfully. If a destination cannot process the full export, use a smaller grid or tile size, or confirm the destination's supported dimensions before exporting.

What happens if I delete Mozaik?

Deleting Mozaik removes its locally prepared tiles, color information, cache, and app settings. It does not delete your original photos or mosaics and videos already saved to the Photo Library. If you reinstall the app, you will need to sync your selected photos again. Eligible subscriptions can be restored through your Apple Account.
