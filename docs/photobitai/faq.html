Help with PhotoBitAI

Frequently asked questions

Getting started with layers, editing tools, and exporting your compositions.

What is PhotoBitAI?

PhotoBitAI is a simple, layer-based photo editor for combining backgrounds, photos, extracted subjects, and text into creative compositions.

How do layers work?

Each layer contains a separate photo, object, or piece of text. You can position, resize, rotate, hide, replace, and edit layers independently.

Can PhotoBitAI remove image backgrounds?

Yes. You can isolate subjects using Apple’s Subject Lift technology or the app’s built-in object-detection model.

Can I refine an extracted subject?

Yes. The mask refinement tools let you erase unwanted areas or restore missing parts before adding the subject as a layer.

Can I create a custom background?

Yes. You can choose a photo or create a solid-color or gradient background with a custom canvas size.

What editing tools are included?

PhotoBitAI includes cropping, filters, color adjustments, text layers, layer positioning, visibility controls, and customizable drop and ground shadows.

Which export formats are supported?

You can export your finished composition as a JPEG or PNG image.

Where can I save my finished image?

You can save it to your photo library or Files, or share it using other apps installed on your device.

Are my photos uploaded or collected?

No. PhotoBitAI processes your photos directly on your device and does not upload them to external servers.

Does PhotoBitAI require an internet connection?

No. The main photo-editing and image-processing features work locally on your device.
