Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

Your privacy

PhotoBitAI does not collect your personal data.

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • The app does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, usage history, or device identifiers.
  • Photos are processed locally on your device.
  • Photos and editing results are not uploaded to PhotoBitAI or shared with the developer.
  • Images remain in memory only while you edit them and are released when the editing session ends or the app closes.
  • Images are saved or shared only when you explicitly choose an export action.

Third-party services

Purchases are processed by Apple. PhotoBitAI does not receive or store your payment-card information.

Read Apple’s Privacy Policy

When you use the system share sheet, Files, Photos, or another application, the destination you select may process the exported image according to its own privacy policy.

If you contact the developer by email, the developer will receive the email address and information you voluntarily provide. That information will be used only to respond to your message.

Data deletion

PhotoBitAI is serverless and does not operate remote servers or databases. Photos and personal data are not uploaded to or stored by PhotoBitAI, so the developer holds no user data that requires a deletion request.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app’s data practices change. The current policy will remain available inside the app.
